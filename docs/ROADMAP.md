# Roadmap

## Foundation

Repository, architecture, documentation, visual language, and quality gates.

## Core platform

Database, authentication, RLS, memberships, seasons, and configuration.

## League operations

Teams, owners, historical import, finance engine, and ledger.

## Competition

ESPN synchronization, standings, weekly awards, playoffs, and records.

## Product experience

Member dashboard, financial transparency, team pages, history, activity, and
commissioner administration.

## Expansion

Commissioner AI message composer with copy-to-thread handoff, PWA support,
generated recaps, trade history, waiver analytics, and the golf draft module.
