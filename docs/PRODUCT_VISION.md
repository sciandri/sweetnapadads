# Product vision

Sweet Looking Napa Dads should feel like a private club ledger, record book,
and museum built by someone taking a ridiculous hobby exactly seriously
enough.

The application automates what ESPN does not:

- league finances;
- commissioner workflows;
- durable history;
- configurable awards and penalties;
- records, media, and lore.

Information enters once. ESPN supplies competition facts when possible.
Commissioner input is reserved for configuration, payment records, editorial
content, and explicit corrections. Every derived result remains traceable.

The product should be polished, funny, and trustworthy. Financial information
must favor clarity over jokes; personality belongs in presentation and
editorial content, never in accounting semantics.
